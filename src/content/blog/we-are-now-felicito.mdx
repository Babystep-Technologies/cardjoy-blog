CardJoy is now **Felicito**!

Same product, same mission: making it easy to celebrate the people in your life. New name, new home at [felicito.app](https://felicito.app).

## Why "Felicito"?

*Felicito* is Spanish for "I congratulate", from the verb *felicitar*. Congratulating the people we care about is what a card is for, so the name felt right.

## A new look

The new wordmark spells **felicit** and ends with a sparkle where the final "o" is. It is the same sparkle you know from CardJoy, and it still cycles through our brand colors: green, pink, blue and yellow.

## What Felicito does

Everything CardJoy did, and nothing was removed:

- **Group cards**: collect messages from everyone in one place
- **1-on-1 cards**: a personal note for one person
- **Invitations with RSVP**: invite guests and see who is coming
- **Registries**: share what you would love to receive
- **Slack integration**: start a group card right from Slack

## What changes for you

- **The name and the address.** CardJoy is now Felicito, and cardjoy.app is now [felicito.app](https://felicito.app). The blog has moved to [blog.felicito.app](https://blog.felicito.app).
- **The Slack command.** Use `/felicito` from now on. The old `/cardjoy` keeps working for a transition period, so you have time to switch.
- **Emails.** Messages from us now show Felicito as the sender name. The sending address has not changed yet, so there is nothing to update in your filters.

## What stays the same

- **Your account, cards and invitations.** Nothing is lost and nothing needs to be redone. Sign in the same way you always did.
- **Your old links.** Links to cardjoy.app forward to the same page on felicito.app, so cards and invitations you have already shared will still open.
- **What it is.** Felicito is still open source, and still built to make life's moments feel more special.

## Try it

Head to [felicito.app](https://felicito.app) to sign in, make a card, or send an invitation. If you use Slack, try `/felicito` in any channel.

Thank you for being part of the CardJoy chapter. We are excited for this next one as Felicito.
